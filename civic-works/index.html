The Civic Works Series

The projects we intend to see built. Each file is a buildable proposal with a site, a program, and a path to execution; MEMBERS files are visible to intelligence subscribers.

  • ✑ The Seattle Grand Prix MOTORSPORT Formula 1 turned Miami and Las Vegas into the biggest civic events in America and put street circuits on the global map. Seattle has the stadium district, the light rail, and a waterfront backdrop no American circuit can match. A SoDo and stadium-district street race puts Seattle on the calendar next to Monaco and Singapore.
  • ✑ MEMBERS A Washington for Washington MONUMENT The Lenin at the Fremont intersection has been for sale for thirty years; the removal everyone argues about was only ever missing a buyer with a better idea for the metal. In 1776 the Republic melted a king into musket balls. Seven tons of bronze, the state that carries the name, and the 250th year.
  • ✑ MEMBERS Lake City Corridor MIXED-USE Mixed-use development along NE 125th in 10 phases.
  • ✑ Second Fremont Bridge Proposal BRIDGES The most-opened drawbridge in America carries a million bike rides a year on six-foot sidewalks, and the only idea on the table is rationing it. Build its twin instead: a bike and pedestrian bascule on the east side of the Fremont Bridge, same 1917 style, same blue and orange.
  • ✑ The Elliott Bay Fishing Pier WATERFRONT Expedia built a 40-acre campus on Elliott Bay at Smith Cove and opened its shoreline to the public, the best stretch of working-waterfront beach downtown. The old Port fishing pier a half mile south proves what is missing: a place to walk out over the water and cast a line. Build a proper public fishing and gathering pier next to the one good place on the water, in the civic style.
