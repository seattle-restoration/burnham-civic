Washington State
  • ⚖ WA Supreme Court Income Tax Case (2026) SUPREME COURT Five of nine Washington Supreme Court seats are on the November 2026 ballot. The court reads seven to two against the 1933 Culliton rule that blocks an income tax. Three seats decide whether the wall stands. The slate to keep it: Bloom, O'Donnell, Larson, Edwards, Stevens. Burnham Civic is building a Committee of Correspondence to rally turnout. Research at culliton2026.org.
  • ⚖ Washington Department of Revenue DOR This is the agency that would collect the new 9.9% income tax on AGI over $1M, if Culliton falls. The full brief covers its history, structure, and the legislative paper trail behind ESSB 6346. Password preview: saveseattle.
